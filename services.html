Services

Personal, independent help with your watches

Our guides are free for everyone. When you'd like advice about your own watches, these sessions give you time with someone who knows them well — with no sales pitch attached.

01
For first-time & returning buyers

Independent buying guidance

A relaxed video or in-person conversation about what you want from a watch — how you'll wear it, the size that suits you, movement types, water resistance and realistic care costs. You leave with a clear checklist of what to look for and what to ask any seller.

  • We never sell watches or earn commissions
  • Fit and sizing advice using your wrist measurements
  • A written summary after the session
Enquire
02
For owners, groups & clubs

Care & patina workshops

Small, hands-on sessions covering everyday care: setting and winding safely, cleaning a case and bracelet, understanding bronze patina, and recognizing the signs that a watch needs a professional service.

  • Online or in person in the New York area
  • Practical demonstrations you can repeat at home
  • Printed care notes for every participant
Enquire
03
For anyone refreshing a watch

Strap & fit sessions

We help you measure lug width, choose strap materials and lengths that suit your wrist and lifestyle, and show you how to change straps safely with spring-bar tools.

  • Lug-width and wrist measurements
  • Material pros and cons for your routine
  • Step-by-step strap-changing guidance
Enquire
04
For growing collections

Collection record-keeping

We help you set up a simple, private record of each watch — photographs, reference details, strap sizes, service history and care notes — so everything is organized in one place.

  • A tidy template you keep and own
  • Guidance on photographing watches clearly
  • Reminders for service and battery intervals
Enquire
05
For libraries, clubs & schools

Horology talks

Friendly, illustrated talks on topics such as “How a Mechanical Watch Works”, “The Story of the Chronograph” and “Materials that Age Beautifully”.

  • 45–60 minutes with time for questions
  • Suitable for general audiences
  • Tailored to your group's interests
Enquire

Please note: we don't open, repair or service watches. For servicing, we always recommend a qualified watchmaker or the manufacturer's authorized service center.
