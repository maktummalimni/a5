1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature. Both let a site remember information between visits. This page explains what flybackbronze.com stores and how to change your choice.

4. Consent Mode

We use Google Consent Mode. Before you choose, analytics and advertising storage are set to "denied", so Google tags don't store cookies. If you accept all, those settings change to "granted". If you choose essential only, they stay denied.

5. Our watch tools

The flyback demo, wrist-fit guide, water-resistance decoder and glossary don't use cookies or save what you enter. Refreshing the page resets them.

6. Controlling cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you view, block and delete cookies in their settings. Blocking all cookies won't stop our guides from working, though your cookie choice won't be remembered.
