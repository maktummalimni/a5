1. General information

Everything on Flyback Bronze is written to help you understand watches. It's general in nature and doesn't take into account the specific model, age or condition of your watch. Always follow your manufacturer's instructions first.

2. See a qualified watchmaker

Opening a caseback, replacing gaskets or batteries in water-resistant watches, demagnetizing, regulating or servicing a movement all require specialist tools and training. If a watch is running unusually fast or slow, has moisture under the crystal or has been dropped, please take it to a qualified watchmaker or the manufacturer's authorized service center.

3. Interactive tools

The wrist-fit guide gives typical ranges based on common proportions; personal taste varies. The water-resistance decoder summarizes widely used guidance for each rating, but the real performance of a watch depends on its age, seals and service history. The flyback demo is an illustration, not a timing instrument.

4. Bronze and materials

Bronze alloys differ between manufacturers, and patina develops differently with climate and skin chemistry. Some cleaning methods discussed by owners can remove patina or affect finishes; test carefully and follow the maker's advice. If you notice skin irritation from any metal, stop wearing the watch and seek appropriate advice.

5. Trademarks and independence

Flyback Bronze is independent. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any watch manufacturer, retailer or brand. Any brand or model names are used only for identification and remain the property of their owners. We don't earn commissions or accept payment for coverage.

6. No valuation or authentication

We don't value, appraise or authenticate watches. Nothing on this site should be taken as a statement about a watch's worth or authenticity.
