Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Who we are

Flyback Bronze ("we", "us") publishes free educational guides about wristwatches at flybackbronze.com and offers the independent advisory services described on our Services page. Our postal address is 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States. You can reach us about privacy at hello@flybackbronze.com or +1-888-777-5845.

2. Information we collect

  • Details you send us. When you use the contact or enquiry form, or email us, we receive your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message. If you mention your watches, that information is included too.
  • Newsletter sign-ups. If you join the Bench Notes letter, we keep your email address and the date you subscribed.
  • Usage information. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics records general information such as pages viewed, approximate location (city level), device type and how you arrived at the site.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, including IP address and browser type, for security and troubleshooting.

The wrist-fit guide, water-resistance decoder, flyback demo and glossary run entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to us or stored.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and arrange any service you ask about.
  • To send the newsletter you requested — and nothing else.
  • To understand which guides are useful so we can improve them.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it with watch brands or retailers.

4. Legal bases

Where laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, we rely on your consent (analytics and advertising cookies, newsletter), on steps taken at your request before providing a service, and on our legitimate interest in running a secure, useful website.

5. Google Analytics and advertising

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-0LY0HY7L01) with Google Consent Mode. Analytics and advertising storage stays switched off until you choose "Accept all" in our cookie banner. Google processes this data under its own terms; you can learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

If advertising is shown on this site in future, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on previous visits, but only where you have consented. You can manage personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings.

6. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us operate: our website host, our email provider, any form-handling service we use to deliver messages to our inbox, and Google for analytics. Each is bound to use the data only to provide their service. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

Messages and enquiries are kept for up to 24 months after our last exchange, then deleted. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained for 14 months in Google Analytics.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live — including under California law (CCPA/CPRA) and the GDPR — you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days. You won't be treated differently for exercising these rights. You can change your cookie choice on our Cookie Policy page.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and we limit access to messages to the people who need to answer them. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't send sensitive information such as payment details through our forms.

10. Children

Our site is written for a general adult audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from them; if you believe a child has contacted us, let us know and we'll delete it.

11. International visitors

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process data in the US or other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the date at the top. Significant changes will be highlighted on the home page for a reasonable period.
