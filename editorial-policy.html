Editorial Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Our purpose

Flyback Bronze exists to explain watches clearly and honestly. This policy sets out how we research, write and update our guides so you can judge how much to rely on them.

2. Independence

We don't sell watches, accept free products in exchange for coverage, publish sponsored articles or use affiliate links. No brand reviews our pages before publication. If that ever changes, we'll label it clearly on the page concerned and update this policy.

3. How we research

Our guides draw on established watchmaking knowledge, manufacturers' published specifications and manuals, and recognized standards such as ISO 6425 for dive watches and ISO 22810 for water-resistant watches. We explain these in our own words and link to primary sources where it's useful.

4. Original writing

All text on this site is written for Flyback Bronze. We don't copy other publications' articles, and we don't reproduce copyrighted diagrams or brand marketing material. Photographs are licensed from Pexels.

5. Practical and safe advice

When we describe something you can do at home, we explain the risk and say when a job belongs with a professional watchmaker. We avoid advice that could damage a watch or void a warranty without clearly saying so.

6. Interactive tools

Each tool is based on common, published rules of thumb, and we test it with a range of values before release. We describe its limits on the page and in our Disclaimer.

7. Reviews and corrections

We review our guides at least once a year and whenever standards or common practice change. If you find a mistake, email hello@flybackbronze.com. We fix factual errors promptly and, where a correction changes the meaning of advice, note it on the page.

8. Use of tools and technology

We may use software to check spelling, links and accessibility. Every guide is reviewed and approved by a person before it's published, and we take responsibility for everything that appears on the site.
